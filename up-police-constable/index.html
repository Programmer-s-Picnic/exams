UP Police Constable exam preparation guide

Create a realistic study routine: assess where you stand, choose topics to practise and use mock tests to improve your speed and accuracy.

Start with a diagnostic assessment

Begin with the unlimited free general reasoning diagnostic. Guests do not need an account and can view their scores and feedback straight away. A guest result is shown for that attempt but is not saved on the server.

Learn exactly how the free diagnostic works.

Choose your exam and practise by topic

Register or sign in, select UP Police Constable when it is available in the examination list, and review the approved syllabus and question sets. Choose topics for targeted practice rather than repeating only the questions you already know.

Use timed mock tests for exam-day practice

When a full-length mock is published by the administrator, use its timer, question navigation and review features to practise pacing. Read the test instructions before beginning: timing, feedback and marking rules may vary by paper.

Review mistakes and work on weak areas

Study explanations after each attempt. For supported practice papers you can return to wrong answers or unanswered questions, and shuffle questions and choices during a fresh attempt. Signed-in students can review saved report cards and performance trends across supported devices.

A straightforward weekly routine

  1. Assess: attempt a diagnostic, then identify two topics needing work.
  2. Learn: revise relevant concepts and examples before practising.
  3. Practise: solve published topic-wise questions and review incorrect answers.
  4. Time: take a published timed mock when available.
  5. Review: compare accuracy, speed and weak topics before planning the next week.

Frequently asked questions

Can I prepare without an account?

Yes. Guests may take the general reasoning diagnostic repeatedly without creating an account. Exam-specific practice and saved progress require registration and sign-in.

Are official examination dates shown here?

Always confirm examination dates, eligibility, vacancy information and official rules directly with the responsible government recruitment authority.

Can I practise previous questions and mock papers?

Question sets and mock papers appear when prepared, reviewed and published by the site administrator. Unavailable material is not displayed as a live test.

Important: UP NaukriGuru is an independent exam-preparation platform, not a government recruitment authority. For official syllabus, eligibility, application dates and examination notifications, check the responsible government authority.
