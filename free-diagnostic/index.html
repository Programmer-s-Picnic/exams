Free general reasoning diagnostic test

Find your starting point with an unlimited diagnostic test for guests. See your answers and feedback immediately without registering.

How the guest diagnostic works

  1. Open the free diagnostic on UP NaukriGuru.
  2. Read the on-screen instructions and begin the general reasoning questions.
  3. Submit the completed test and view the score, answer review and recommended focus areas.
  4. Retake the guest diagnostic as often as you like to practise further.
Guest privacy: Guest reports are shown for the current attempt only. They are not saved as a permanent server-side student record. Save any personal notes before leaving the report screen.

What your result tells you

The report is a learning aid. Review correct and incorrect answers, revisit topics that need attention and plan your next practice session. A diagnostic score does not predict an official recruitment examination result.

What changes when you sign in?

Registered students can select an available examination, take diagnostics aligned with that exam, and review multiple completed attempts with randomized questions and MCQ choice orders where supported. Signed-in report cards can be saved to the platform's server and accessed from supported devices.

Continue your preparation

For exam-specific practice, see our UP Police Constable preparation guide, then register to choose an exam and explore published papers. You can also use the Android app.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free diagnostic really unlimited?

Yes. Guests may attempt the general reasoning diagnostic as many times as they wish without logging in.

Will my guest test result be saved?

No. Guest results and answers are displayed for that attempt and are not stored as permanent server-side student records.

How is a signed-in diagnostic different?

Signed-in diagnostics follow the student's selected available examination, can randomize questions and choices between attempts, and save completed report cards to the account.

Does a diagnostic score predict the official exam result?

No. It is a preparation and feedback tool, not a prediction of an official examination outcome.

Important: UP NaukriGuru is an independent exam-preparation platform, not a government recruitment authority. For official syllabus, eligibility, application dates and examination notifications, check the responsible government authority.
