Preparing for a competitive examination takes consistency, useful practice and feedback you can act on. UP NaukriGuru brings these elements together on the web and in a dedicated Android student app.

Begin with a free diagnostic

Start with the free diagnostic to assess your current preparation. Review your score, accuracy, subject-wise and topic-wise performance, and recommended focus areas. A diagnostic is a preparation tool—not a prediction of an official examination result.

Practise the way you learn best

Move through topic-based question sets and mock tests. Where a test allows it, practise with immediate feedback or review explanations and results after submitting the whole paper. Timed tests help build exam-day pace; untimed practice helps you understand topics carefully.

Retake questions that deserve another look

Return to unanswered questions, wrong answers, or the full set for another attempt. Supported practice tests shuffle questions and options on retakes, helping you focus on understanding instead of memorising their order.

Track progress across devices

Review previous attempts, scores and detailed answers. Signed-in users can sync supported completed test results through the platform's server and access their preparation history from the website or app. Active in-progress attempts can be resumed on the device where they were saved.

Relevant content, reviewed before publication

Exam content includes syllabus information, approved question sets and previous-paper material where available and validated by the site administrator. Unreleased examinations remain labelled as coming soon.

Getting started is simple

  1. Download and install the Android app, or open the website.
  2. Register or sign in to your student account.
  3. Select UP Police Constable as your examination focus.
  4. Take the free diagnostic, practise questions and review your progress.
  5. Return regularly to target weaker subjects and improve your accuracy.
